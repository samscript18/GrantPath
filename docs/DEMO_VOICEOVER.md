# GrantPath demo: what to say

About three minutes, with pauses for clicks. Read the words below naturally. The matching screen actions are in [DEMO.md](DEMO.md).

## 1. Welcome

Finding a scholarship can be overwhelming. There are so many websites, different requirements, and deadlines to keep track of.

GrantPath helps students find opportunities, understand what they need, and keep their applications organised—all in one place.

Let me show you how it works.

## 2. Find a scholarship

This is the scholarship page. I can search for an opportunity and narrow the results by where I want to study, my study level, and the type of funding.

I'll search for GLOCAL.

This programme offers study across different countries. Whether I find it through Germany or Japan, I can open the same opportunity and see its participating universities.

When I open a scholarship, I can see what the funding covers and what the provider says about applying. I can also follow the official links to check the information myself.

That helps me understand the opportunity before deciding whether to pursue it.

## 3. Understand your match

To make the results more personal, I fill in my study profile.

This includes my citizenship, where I live, what I'm studying now, and what I want to study next.

GrantPath uses that information to explain how I match the scholarship's requirements.

I can see what I meet, what I don't meet, and what I still need to confirm. If something is missing, the app tells me instead of assuming I qualify.

It also keeps the application window separate, so I can check whether the opportunity is currently open.

## 4. Plan an application

If an opportunity interests me, I can save it.

Here, I can add a private note and use a checklist to keep track of what I need to prepare.

Then I can open My applications and update my progress—for example, to Preparing documents.

When I return later, my saved work is still here. I can see where I left off and what to do next.

## 5. Stay informed

The inbox brings scholarship alerts into the same workspace.

I can open an alert, read what it is about, and return to the scholarship for more information.

In settings, I can choose which notifications I want to receive.

## 6. Finish

GrantPath brings the scholarship journey together: finding an opportunity, understanding the requirements, and preparing an application.

It helps students spend less time keeping track of scattered information and more time taking their next step.

This is GrantPath.
